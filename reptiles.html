Komodo Dragon

The **Komodo dragon** is the largest living species of lizard, growing up to 10 feet (3 meters) in length. Native to a few Indonesian islands, it has a muscular build, rough skin, and a powerful tail. Komodo dragons are carnivorous and can take down large prey like deer. They have a venomous bite that prevents blood clotting in their victims.

Green Iguana

The **green iguana** is a large, arboreal lizard found in Central and South America. It has a long tail, sharp claws, and a crest of spines running down its back. Iguanas are primarily herbivores, feeding on leaves, flowers, and fruit. They are excellent climbers and swimmers and are commonly kept as exotic pets

Reptile

Leopard Gecko

Native to the arid regions of Pakistan and India, the leopard gecko is a small, nocturnal reptile known for its spotted pattern and fat tail. Unlike most geckos, it has movable eyelids. It feeds on insects and small invertebrates. Easy to care for, it is one of the most popular reptiles in the pet trade.
