Marine Life: Mammals

Dolphin

The **dolphin** is a smart, playful marine mammal known for its sleek body, curved dorsal fin, and friendly nature. Found in oceans and some rivers, dolphins live in groups called pods and use echolocation and whistles to communicate and navigate.

Mammal

Orca (Killer Whale)

The **orca whale**, also known as the **killer whale**, is a large, black-and-white marine mammal known for its intelligence, strength, and complex social behavior. Found in oceans worldwide, orcas are top predators and live in pods, using coordinated hunting techniques and vocal communication.

Mammal

Beluga Whale

The **beluga whale** is a small, white whale known for its rounded forehead and lack of a dorsal fin. Native to Arctic and sub-Arctic waters, belugas are highly social, vocal marine mammals often called "canaries of the sea" due to their wide range of sounds.
