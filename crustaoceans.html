Marine Life: Crustaceans

Crab

Crabs are short-bodied crustaceans with a thick exoskeleton and a pair of pincers. They live in oceans, freshwater, and even on land.warm shallow waters of the Pacific and Indian Oceans, including the Great Barrier Reef and the Red Sea Clownfish are famous for their symbiotic relationship with sea anemones, where they find protection among the stinging tentacles that would harm most other fish

Lobster

Lobsters are large marine crustaceans with long bodies, powerful claws, and live on ocean floors. They are also a seafood delicacy warm shallow waters of the Pacific and Indian Oceans, including the Great Barrier Reef and the Red Sea Clownfish are famous for their symbiotic relationship with sea anemones, where they find protection among the stinging tentacles that would harm most other fish.
